# Install

Download ADHD Music from https://adhdmusic.app/download, open the .dmg and drag ADHD Music into Applications. Open ADHD Music and it appears in your menu bar. macOS asks once for System Audio Recording: allow it, and the music pauses while a YouTube video plays. Nothing is recorded. The app updates itself quietly from then on. ADHD Music needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
