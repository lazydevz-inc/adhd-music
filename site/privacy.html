ADHD Music

Privacy

Updated November 25, 2026

ADHD Music has no account and no cookies, and collects nothing that identifies you. The app keeps your history and settings on your Mac. This website measures how its page is used, so we can make it better.

In the app

  • Your history, tasks, likes, skips and settings stay on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere.
  • macOS asks once for System Audio Recording. The app uses it only to tell whether another app, like a YouTube video, is playing sound, so it can pause and play again. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • The version downloaded from this website checks https://adhdmusic.app about once a day for a new version. That request carries the app's version number and nothing about you. We count these checks per day, by app version and country, to know roughly how many people use the app. No IP address or identifier is kept.

On this website

  • Vercel Web Analytics counts visits: page views, the referring site, country and device type, in aggregate.
  • PostHog shows us how the page is used: which sections are seen, which buttons are pressed, how much of the video plays, and recordings of the screen during a visit, with anything typed hidden. Your country is estimated from your IP address, which is then discarded.
  • Nothing is stored in your browser: no cookies and no local storage. A visit is not linked to your next visit or to other sites.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel and the download is served by GitHub, which keep standard server logs.
