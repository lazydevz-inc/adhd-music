ADHD Music

Privacy

Updated November 25, 2026

ADHD Music collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, in the app or on this website.

In the app

  • Your history, tasks, likes, skips and settings stay on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • macOS asks once for System Audio Recording. The app uses it only to tell whether another app, like a YouTube video, is playing sound, so it can pause and play again. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • The version downloaded from this website checks https://adhdmusic.app about once a day for a new version. That request carries the app's version number and nothing about you.

On this website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no outside fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on Vercel and the download is served by GitHub, which keep standard server logs.
